# 🍽️ Zomato Data Analysis Using Python

# 📌 Introduction
The Zomato dataset provides insights into restaurant trends, including online vs. offline services, customer preferences for restaurant types, and pricing patterns for couples. This report aims to answer the following questions:
1.	Do more restaurants offer online delivery than offline services?
2.	What types of restaurants are most favored by the general public?
3.	What price range is preferred by couples for their dinner at restaurants?
# 📊 Dataset Overview
The dataset consists of multiple attributes related to restaurants, including:
•	Restaurant Type: Categories such as Casual Dining, Quick Bites, Cafes, etc.
•	Online Order Availability: Whether the restaurant offers online delivery services.
•	Ratings & Votes: Customer ratings and number of votes.
# 🛠 Data Cleaning & Processing

•	Missing Values Check: There are no missing values in the dataset.

•	Duplicate Records Check: No duplicate values were found in the dataset.

•	The data was cleaned and visualized using Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn).

# Analysis & Insights
# 1. Online Delivery vs Offline Services
A count plot of online order availability shows that a higher number of restaurants provide online delivery than those that do not. This suggests that restaurants are adapting to increasing demand for online food services.

•	Insight: The majority of restaurants support online delivery.
# 2. Most Popular Restaurant Types
A count plot of different restaurant types indicates that Casual Dining and Quick Bites are the most preferred categories by the public, followed by Cafes and Dessert Parlors.

•	Insight: Casual Dining and Quick Bites receive the highest footfall and orders.
# 3. Preferred Price Range for Couples
A count plot of the approximate cost for two people suggests that most couples prefer restaurants with a moderate price range rather than luxury dining options.

•	Insight: Couples mostly prefer mid-range dining experiences.
# 4. Correlation Between Online Orders and Ratings
A box plot comparing online order availability with restaurant ratings shows that restaurants offering online delivery tend to have higher ratings than those without online services.

•	Insight: Online service availability correlates with better ratings, possibly due to wider customer reach.
# 5. Heatmap of Online Orders vs. Restaurant Type
A heatmap analysis shows the relationship between restaurant types and online order availability, confirming that Quick Bites and Cafes are more likely to offer online delivery.

•	Insight: Quick Bites and Cafes dominate online orders, while Fine Dining restaurants lean towards offline services.
# 📈 Visualizations Used
📌 Countplots for analyzing online order trends and restaurant types.

📌 Boxplots to compare restaurant ratings with online order availability.

📌 Heatmaps to study correlations between restaurant types & online orders.
# Key Findings
📌 Online Orders Dominate: More restaurants offer online delivery than offline services.

📌 Most Popular Restaurant Types: Casual Dining & Quick Bites attract the highest customer engagement.

📌 Price Preferences for Couples: Most couples prefer mid-range dining over luxury restaurants.

📌 Online Orders & Ratings: Restaurants with online delivery tend to have higher ratings.
# ✅ Conclusion
The analysis provides valuable insights into customer preferences, restaurant trends, and pricing behaviors. The findings suggest that online delivery services are a significant factor in restaurant success, and mid-range pricing is preferred by couples. Future analysis could explore customer sentiment analysis through reviews or predictive modeling for restaurant success.
